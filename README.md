Woodgrove Ads – Microsoft Advertising Sales Executive Dashboard
Role being mimicked
Sales Operations Data Analyst – Microsoft Advertising

As the Sales Operations Data Analyst, you build and maintain executive-ready dashboards and reports, pulling and shaping data across systems and turning complex data models into clear, usable insights for leaders.

Business scenario
You work for Woodgrove Ads, which sells Microsoft Advertising inventory (Search, Display, Video) to enterprise customers. Your stakeholders are:

VP Sales, Advertising – needs high-level revenue and pipeline KPIs.
​

Regional Sales Directors – need performance by region/rep.
​

Marketing Lead – cares about campaign performance and ROAS.

Business goals
Track booked revenue and pipeline vs quarterly targets.

Understand which ad products, regions, and accounts drive growth.

Forecast end-of-quarter revenue from the current pipeline.

Core questions the dashboard must answer
Are we on track to hit quarterly revenue targets?

Which regions and accounts are over- or under-performing?
​

How healthy is the pipeline (coverage, win rate, cycle length)?

Users and required views
Executives: top-level, minimal clutter, one-screen overview.
​

Sales managers: ability to drill down by region, team, and rep.
​

Marketing: campaign performance and ROAS at account/product level.

Tech stack
Data generation: Python.
​

Storage: CSVs (with option to later move to SQL).
​

BI: Power BI Desktop (semantic model + reports)
